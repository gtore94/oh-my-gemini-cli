# Skill: omc_trace

## Purpose
Evidence-driven causal tracing to explain WHY an observed result happened.

## Core Protocol
- **Observation**: What was actually seen.
- **Hypotheses**: 3 deliberately different competing explanations.
- **Evidence**: Gather "Evidence For" and "Evidence Against" for each.
- **Synthesis**: Rank the explanations, identify the "Critical Unknown", and propose a "Discriminating Probe".
